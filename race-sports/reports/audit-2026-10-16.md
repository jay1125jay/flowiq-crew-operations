## Horse confidence research

Audit found date/lexical-ID ordering could train on later same-day results, odds availability filtered the cohort before splitting, changing backfill could silently retune the rules, and a negative-discovery rule could receive a passing label based on holdout alone.

Repairs: predict an entire date before learning its results; round warmup and 60/40 split to full-date boundaries; retain predictions lacking odds and exclude them only from ROI; finite odds only; predetermined 1,785-rule grid; select primary rule using discovery only; persist cohort IDs, input hash and rules.
